DBMS Architecture Notice

ACID
Atomic Transaction Processing

Your transfer updates both balances and creates transfer records in a single atomic transaction. If either debit or credit fails, Oracle issues an automatic ROLLBACK.

LOCK
Deadlock-Free Row Level Locks

Accounts are locked in strictly ascending numeric order (min(acc1, acc2) followed by max(acc1, acc2)) via SELECT FOR UPDATE to mathematically eliminate cyclic wait conditions.

SAFE
Backend Authorization Barrier

The server enforces customer ID isolation on the database query. You cannot transfer funds out of accounts not belonging to your authenticated login.
